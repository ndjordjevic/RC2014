## New RC2014 Website 

Converting the RC2014 website from WordPress to a static website had been on the To Do List for quite a while. The PHPocolypse last week suddenly moved it to the top of the list. For unknown reasons, the site wouldn't run properly under PHP 8.x and running on PHP 7.4 was ruled out, so the conversion has been rushed through a bit. Things aren't totally done, and there are still a lot of broken links, but it is up and running now.  
A static website should mean much quicker response times, a cleaner layout and no data saved on the website. Smaller page sizes will use less data too. It also features light mode and dark mode.  
Sorry to everybody that found the site down or giving errors last week, and thank you to everybody that let me know.

## US Shipping 

Oh buoy, what a rollercoaster we're on.  Earlier this month new US regulations were due to come in to effect that would have made it untenable to ship kits to the US. Rather than be buried in many hours of paperwork per US order, I had to put shipping on hold. At the last minute, they were moved back, and I resumed shipping. However, it looks like they have just been moved back to January 2027. As a small independent trader, I do not have the time or resources to supply all the documentation required for every single component in every single kit sent to the US. So unless the new regulations are significantly altered, or exemptions put in place beforehand**I will be unable to ship kits to the US after December**. If you are visiting friends or relatives outside the US I will be very happy to ship there for you to collect.

## RC2014 Assembly 2.0

  
Wow, what a great event the [second RC2014 Assembly](https://rc2014.co.uk/rc2014-assembly-v2-0/) was! [The National Museum of Computing](https://www.tnmoc.org/) were excellent hosts again and there was a fine selection of exhibitors and traders there. Sadly the talks weren't recorded, but the talk by Roman Iwanczuk, a former Field Application Engineer at Zilog was a personal highlight. Looking forward to 3.0!

# Community Support

Got a question about the RC2014? A project you want to share with other RC2014 users?  Need to find some software that runs on your RC2014?  Or just want to see what other RC2014 owners are up to?  
  
[Google Groups](https://groups.google.com/forum/#!forum/rc2014-z80) and [GitHub](https://github.com/RC2014Z80/RC2014) are starting to become vibrant parts of the RC2014 infrastructure.  Why not join up today?  No Google account needed!
