## RC2014 Assembly - One month away 

It is only a month until [RC2014 Assembly 2.0](https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/rc2014-assembly-20-tickets-1992559683652), and things are shaping up to be another fantastic event.  The National Museum of Computing in Bletchley Park are pleased to be hosting us again, and we're looking forward to going back. There's already some great people putting their name forward for the 10-15 minute Lightning Talks, including another international video link-up. If you have something people may find interesting or entertaining, please sign up on the day.  
This years name badge has been ordered from JLCPCB again, so we're just waiting for delivery of those. It was hard to beat last years design, but hopefully we've done that this time.  
If you haven't got your ticket yet, there are still Trader, Exhibitor and Attendee tickets available.

## Mini Zed Storage Layers

To compliment the RC2014 Mini Zed, two new storage layers will be available from the start of September.  
The first takes a [1.8" HDD](https://z80kits.com/shop/1-8-ide-hdd-layer-for-mini-zed/) as found in the iPod Classics. Using a real spinning disc hard drive in an RC2014 really adds to the experience.  
|  |  ![](https://mcusercontent.com/9794c5b78f76285b2bc1ff7bd/images/c26244e2-2e41-6231-04bb-fb22af4ef2b0.jpg)  
---  
And the other is based on the Mini II CP/M Upgrade Kit, but with just the [compact flash ](https://z80kits.com/shop/compact-flash-layer-for-mini-zed/)circuitry fitted.  
  
These stackable layers lets your RC2014 Mini Zed grow without taking up any more desk space

## RC2014 and z80kits AI Policy 

Both the [RC2014](https://rc2014.co.uk/ai-policy/) and [z80kits](https://z80kits.com/ai-policy/) websites have been updated with an AI policy. In short, **no AI has been used in the design, imagery, code, marketing or supply of your RC2014 kit**.  
Whilst some people enjoy mixing AI with their retro computing hobby, it seems like the majority of people do not. With this in mind, if posting AI generated content or code to the [RC2014 Google Group](https://groups.google.com/g/rc2014-z80/), please label it clearly so people can choose to engage with it or not.

## Orton 3C Code Update 

Following on from the work Shiela Dixon has done with creating [music on the Orton 3C](https://newstuffforoldstuff.blogspot.com/2026/04/using-speccy-beep-routine-and-beeper.html), the music routines have now been included in the ROM. All kits now come with the new code, but you can update your ROM with the code on [Codeberg](https://codeberg.org/RC2014/RC2014_Orton_3C_Code).   
If you don't have a way to write the ROM, [z80kits has you covered](https://z80kits.com/shop/39sf010-programmer-rom-writer-for-orton-3c/) ;-)
